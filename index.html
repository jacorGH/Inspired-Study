How to use Berea
Translations
Public-domain translations download once for full offline reading and search. Licensed translations (NIV, NKJV) aren't included — see the note in the page source for why. YLT covers the New Testament only.
Cross-references (AI-assisted)
Get a ready-made prompt for any AI chat you already use — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, whatever. Copy it in, paste the reply back, and turn what looks useful into a tab. No account or API key required.
Hebrew & Greek (Strong's Concordance)
Look up the original-language word behind a KJV term — free, one-time downloads (Strong's Dictionaries of Hebrew and Greek, public domain).
Tap-a-word text
Download a Strong's-tagged Bible to tap any word while reading and see the Hebrew or Greek behind it. Then use the "Strong's" button at the top of the Read view. One large download each — best on Wi-Fi.
Traditional cross-references (Treasury of Scripture Knowledge)
The classic, non-AI cross-reference set scholars have used for over a century — reachable per-verse from the verse menu once downloaded. It's a large one-time download (several hundred thousand entries), best on Wi-Fi.
Topical study (Nave's Topical Bible)
Study by subject instead of by passage — over 5,000 headings, each with the verses that speak to it. Search them under Search › Topics once downloaded.
Backup & restore
Your tabs, highlights, and notes live only in this browser — clearing site data or switching devices loses them. Save a backup file to keep them safe or move them somewhere else. Downloaded scripture text isn't included (it's re-downloadable).
Apologetics practice
Common objections, framed fairly, with a response outline and the passages to back it up. Good for rehearsing before a real conversation.
